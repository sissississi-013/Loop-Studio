# Open video studio direction

Saved September 13, 2026 after the user reported missing the hackathon submission deadline. This supersedes the hackathon objective for new product decisions; the completed demo and research evidence remain intact.

## User's priorities

- Build toward a useful, functional video-editing platform that could be launched and open sourced.
- Keep base video editing open source; let people plug in their own models. Optional paid packages are acceptable, but their boundary is not yet decided.
- Research competing products and YC video companies comprehensively before deciding what to build. User named FLORA, “Lumain AI,” “Oscmos Studio,” and Palmier. Luma is a possible match for “Lumain,” not confirmed; the other uncertain name remains unresolved.
- Reduce friction and financial commitment before users can judge quality. The user has avoided competing tools because setup/payment is a barrier and they do not trust the output will be worthwhile.
- Accept a few raw video clips, understand their contents, select strong highlight segments, follow ordinary-language instructions, and optionally use seed/reference videos.
- Library integrations or an exposed agent workflow should not be prerequisites for this entry flow.
- Deliver strong aesthetics: deliberate visual design, pacing, composition, typography, music and an output the creator can shape to their intention. Pinterest is an example of communicating aesthetic taste, not authorization to scrape or redistribute its contents.
- Prioritize what people use over recursively improving agents. RL may fit the long-term vision but is not important for the MVP.

## Research delivered

[Market and MVP report](market-and-mvp.md) covers eleven YC companies (including infrastructure and historical comparators), ten additional products/projects, pricing friction, evidence limitations, aesthetics, current-code gaps and a proposed implementation sequence. Competitor capabilities are documented/advertised observations, not common-footage hands-on benchmark results.

The recommendation is a complete first-cut workflow: originals and proxies → temporal understanding → candidate moments → editable style/sequence plan → shared timeline → targeted revisions → original-quality export. Preserve the reliable parts of CutLoop while replacing hackathon limits and fragmented workflows.

## Open choices and recommendations

The first audience has not been confirmed. Aesthetic personal/travel/lifestyle shorts are the working research assumption; product/brand video and speech-led highlights are alternatives. A clarification was sent while research continued.

Ten input clips, ten minutes of total footage, 15–60-second output and 1080p SDR are proposed initial engineering targets, not current capabilities or user-approved final limits. Local and remote providers need concrete supported capability profiles. Exact paid package, license and launch distribution remain undecided.

The user is gathering their own references. The most useful next inputs are actual desired edits and the specific qualities they like, followed by source clips that can test those qualities.

## Execution state

Only research and context documents changed in this turn. No product rewrite, model training, external publication, account signup or paid subscription was performed. The earlier overnight goal remains completed and its heartbeat paused. The existing app/demo and original evaluation artifacts were preserved.
